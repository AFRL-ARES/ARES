Stepper Motor protocol:
ans = ((target >> 7) & 1) | ((target >> 14) & 2) | ((target >> 21) & 4) | ((target >> 28) & 8), target >> 0 & 0x7F, target >> 8 & 0x7F, target >> 16 & 0x7F, target >> 24 & 0x7F
https://www.pololu.com/docs/0J71/all#12.7

Servo Protocol:
https://www.robotshop.com/media/files/PDF/manual-drs-0201.pdf
header: ff ff
packet size
pID 01
CMD
Checksum 1: PacketSize ^ pID ^ CMD ^ Data[0] ^ Data[1] ^ …… ^ Data[n]) & 0xFE (^ is xor)
Checksum 2: ( ~CheckSum1) & 0xFE
Data[n]
ff ff 07 01 07 00 fe // status
ff ff 07 01 09 0e f0 // reboot
ff ff 0c 01 05 ce 30 f4 01 00 01 32 // move to (jumppos=500, take 560 ms)
ff ff 0c 01 05 76 88 4e 02 00 01 32 // piston down (downpos=024e, take 560 ms)
ff ff 0c 01 05 da 24 e0 01 00 01 32 // piston up (uppos=01e0, take 560 ms)
ff ff 0c 01 05 0a f4 00 00 02 01 00 // torque off (set pwm 0 with infinite turn mode)
0xFF, 0xFF, 0x09, 0x01, 0x04, 0x34, 0xCA, 0x3A, 0x02 // Get Position (I think)
